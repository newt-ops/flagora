# Flagora 2.0 — Vision & Roadmap

This document outlines the strategic roadmap, architectural evolution, and feature milestones planned for **Flagora v2.0**.

---

## 🎯 Strategic Goals for v2.0
With Flagora v1.0 successfully establishing the core flag-guessing loop, real-time multiplayer battles, and native Telegram economy, Flagora 2.0 focuses on **depth, social virality, audio immersion, and expanded geography game modes**.

---

## 🗺️ Flagora 2.0 Roadmap Pillars

### 1. 🎵 Immersive Audio & Haptic Engine
* **Web Audio Sound FX Engine**: Lightweight, synthesised sound effects for:
  * Correct answer chime (ascending pitch per combo multiplier)
  * Incorrect answer dull tap
  * Heartbeat / ticking clock in the final 5 seconds
  * Victory fanfare on game complete & level up
* **Audio Settings**: Native toggle for Sound Effects & Haptics in the Player Profile settings.

### 2. 🏛️ Expanded Geography Game Modes
* **Capital Cities Mode**: Match flags or country names to their official capital cities.
* **Country Silhouette / Outline Quiz**: Guess the country based on its geographic border map silhouette.
* **Coat of Arms & National Symbols**: High-difficulty mastery mode for country emblems and official seals.
* **Survival / Sudden Death Mode**: Play with 3 lives; infinite questions with escalating timer pressure per question.

### 3. 🛡️ Guilds, Teams & Country Factions
* **Geography Guilds**: Players can create or join guilds (up to 50 members) with custom guild badges and banners.
* **Weekly Guild Wars**: Aggregated member XP and multiplayer battle wins feed into a seasonal Guild Leaderboard with shared Star and Pin prize pools.
* **Country Factions**: Represent your home country on the Global Country Leaderboard (e.g. "Which nation knows flags best?").

### 4. 🏆 Live Bracket Tournaments (Knockout Cups)
* **8-Player & 16-Player Single Elimination**: Scheduled hourly/daily live tournament brackets.
* **Spectator Mode**: Eliminated players or friends can watch ongoing finals in real-time.
* **Trophy Cabinet**: Exclusive gold, silver, and bronze tournament cup trophies displayable on player profiles.

### 5. 🌍 Internationalization (i18n) & Localized Country Names
* **Multi-Language Support**: Complete translations for English, Spanish, Arabic, French, German, Russian, Chinese, and Portuguese.
* **Localized Answer Choices**: Country names displayed in the player's selected language.

### 6. 🤖 Autonomous Telegram Bot Retention Loop
* **Streak Expiration Warnings**: Telegram push alerts 2 hours before daily streak reset.
* **Challenge Notifications**: Direct message from the bot when a friend sends or beats your 1v1 challenge score.
* **Weekly League Digest**: Summary card showing rating changes, leaderboard rank, and weekly pin earnings.

### 7. 🎨 Visual Polish & Apple UI Evolution
* **Particle & Confetti Engine**: Native canvas particles on personal bests and tier promotions.
* **3D Interactive Globe / Map Explorer**: Interactive 3D globe visualization in the Practice screen allowing players to tap continents and countries to practice individual flags.
